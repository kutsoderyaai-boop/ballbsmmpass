# 🎾 .ballball. — SMM & Community Zine Playbook

> **Brand Name:** `.ballball.` (always written lowercase with dots)  
> **Product:** Smart padel matchmaking app + Urban Community Zine  
> **Primary Location:** Barcelona (Poblenou, Gràcia, Eixample) → Expanding across Europe (Madrid, Milan, Amsterdam, Berlin)

---

## 🎯 1. Core Narrative & Positioning

### The Problem vs. Our Solution
* **The Playtomic Reality:** Playtomic is the elephant in the room for booking courts. We don't pretend it doesn't exist. But searching for games there and scrolling endless Telegram chats takes forever.
* **Our Core Shift:** The old way: *You search → You waste time*. **.ballball. way:** *We search → You get notified.* We turn search on its head: you don't hunt for games; we find them for you and ping you when your match is ready.

### The Philosophy
* **Core Hook:** *"Finding a good game takes time. We don't think it should."*
* **The Perfect Match Equation:** `Your Level + Your Vibe = Your Best Game`.
* **Perfect Fit:** It's **NEVER** just about skill level. It's about skill + shared interests + vibe + playing style.
* **The Padel Truth:** *"Honestly? Padel is just an excuse to meet cool people."*

---

## 🗣️ 2. Tone of Voice & Writing Guidelines

### Character & Tone
* **Vibe:** Effortlessly cool insider from the Barcelona creative community. Work, sport, nightlife, and specialty coffee flow as one seamless stream.
* **Zero Toxicity:** Safe space. *"Zero Experience = Zero Problem"*. No elitism, no pushiness, no corporate fitness hype.
* **Observational Wit:** We notice user patterns without being creepy: *"Still checking the chats?"*, *"You keep choosing the same club. We noticed."*

### Editorial Rules & Fonts
* **Fonts:** PT Serif & SF Pro (Fallback: Google Sans Flex).
* **Brand Name:** Always `.ballball.` (lowercase, enclosed in dots).
* **Language:** Native English for public SMM (Barcelona & European audience).
* **Strict Banned Words:** `delve`, `testament`, `tapestry`, `game-changer`, `revolutionary`, `cutting-edge`.

---

## 👥 3. Target Audience & Personas

### Demographics
* **Age & Vibe:** Progressive urbanites, founders, designers, IT specialists, DJs, lifestyle creators (20–35 yo).
* **Key Segments:**
  1. **Newbies (1–2 games):** Scared of looking dumb. *Message: Come solo, zero judgment.*
  2. **Regulars (1+ games/week):** Tired of chat noise. *Message: Forget scrolling. Your match is ready in 2 clicks.*
  3. **Vibers & Hedonists:** Looking for techno sets, specialty coffee, and cool networking. *Message: Padel is your ticket to a great weekend vibe.*

### Padel Alter-Egos (Mascots)
* **TRYHARD:** Heavy focus on scoring and hustle.
* **PADEL NERD:** Analyzes every racket spec, knows every rule.
* **DOMINATOR:** Owns the net, high energy.
* **EASY RIDER:** Here for the post-game matcha tonic and good vibes.
